What We Do

Our Services

From small repairs to full-scale builds, we bring the same level of care and craftsmanship to every project.

Home Remodeling

Complete kitchen and bathroom renovations, basement finishing, and whole-home remodels. We handle everything from design to final walkthrough.

Custom Builds

New construction and custom home builds tailored to your vision. We work with your architect or provide referrals to trusted design partners.

Additions

Interior Finishing

Decks & Outdoor

Repairs & Maintenance

About Us

Built on Integrity.
Backed by Experience.

Summit Built Construction was founded in 2007 by Mike Dalton, a third-generation carpenter who grew up swinging a hammer alongside his father and grandfather on job sites across Colorado.

Today, our team of 12 skilled tradespeople brings that same family-rooted work ethic to every project. We don't cut corners, we don't disappear mid-project, and we don't leave until the job is done right. That's why over 80% of our work comes from repeat clients and referrals.
